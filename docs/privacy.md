# Crowns privacy policy

_Draft. Host this page somewhere public and set `CROWNS_PRIVACY_URL` in `Config/Crowns.xcconfig`._

Crowns doesn't collect any data about you.

**Your watches** (photos, prices, notes, links) are stored on your device and, if iCloud is on, in your own private iCloud database. We can't see them.

**Saving from Safari.** When you share a page to Crowns, it reads that page on your device to find the watch's details, then downloads its photos from the shop's servers, just as Safari would.

**Exchange rates.** To show prices in pounds, Crowns fetches daily rates from Frankfurter (frankfurter.dev). The request contains no information about you or your watches.

**Purchases** are handled by Apple. We don't receive your payment details.

**On-device intelligence.** When a page has no structured product details, Crowns may use Apple's on-device language model to read it. This happens entirely on your device.

No analytics, no advertising, no tracking.
